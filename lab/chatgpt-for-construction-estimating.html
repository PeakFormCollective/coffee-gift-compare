ChatGPT for Construction Estimating: What It Can and Can't Do

Short answer: ChatGPT is useful for the writing and checklist parts of estimating: scope lists, bid letters, change orders. It is not reliable for quantities or prices. Use it to avoid missing items and to write faster, then put in your own numbers.

Where it saves time

  • Scope checklists: turn a job description into a list of items, materials and labor tasks to price, so fewer things get missed.
  • Bid and proposal letters: clear inclusions, exclusions, assumptions and payment terms, written in a few seconds.
  • Change orders: plain-language descriptions a homeowner can understand and sign.
  • Reading specs: summarize a spec section you paste in, and list the questions to ask before you bid.

Where you can't trust it

  • Takeoffs: don't rely on it to measure quantities from drawings or photos.
  • Prices: it doesn't know your local material prices, your supplier or your labor rate.
  • Code and permits: it can be wrong, and codes vary by location. Check with your local authority.
  • Client data: don't paste confidential client details into a tool whose data terms you haven't checked.
