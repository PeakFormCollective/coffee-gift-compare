Coffee Gift Basket Ideas: What to Put In One

Short answer: a good coffee gift basket has five parts: the coffee, something to drink it from, something to brew it with, a treat, and a card. Match the coffee to how they brew, and the rest is easy.

The 5-part checklist

1. The coffee. One bag is enough. Ground if you don't know whether they own a grinder. Whole bean only if they do. Pods only if you know their machine. Look for a roast date on the bag: fresher is better.
2. Something to drink it from. A mug for home, or an insulated travel mug if they commute.
3. Something to brew it with. Paper filters for their brewer, a small pour-over cone, or a milk frother if they like lattes. Skip this part if they already have a setup they love.
4. A treat. Biscotti, shortbread, chocolate-covered espresso beans or good dark chocolate.
5. A card. One line is enough. Need words? 40 coffee gift card messages.

Three sizes

  • Small: one bag of coffee + a treat + a card. Good for teachers, coworkers and Secret Santa.
  • Medium: coffee + a mug + a treat + a card. The classic.
  • Large: two different coffees (one light, one dark) + a mug + a brewing extra (filters, pour-over cone or frother) + two treats + a card.

Themed basket ideas

  • Morning commute: ground coffee, a travel mug, a granola bar.
  • Weekend slow brew: whole bean coffee, a pour-over cone with filters, shortbread.
  • Latte lover: a dark roast, a handheld milk frother, a flavor syrup.
  • Coffee and chocolate: a medium roast, chocolate-covered espresso beans, dark chocolate.
  • Office thank-you: one bag of ground coffee, a mug, a handwritten card.

Don't know how they make coffee?

Then a basket of beans is a guess. Two safer options: ground medium roast (works with most drip brewers and pour-overs), or a coffee subscription gift, where they choose their own coffee and grind when they redeem it. See how the main coffee subscription gifts compare.

Wrapping it

  • Any box or basket works. Fill the bottom with crinkle paper or a folded dish towel (which is also part of the gift).
  • Put the coffee at the back, the mug in the middle and the small items in front, so everything shows.
  • Keep the card on top so it's the first thing they read.
