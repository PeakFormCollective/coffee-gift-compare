Coffee Gifts for Dad: Pick by the Kind of Coffee Drinker He Is

Short answer: work out which of these five dads yours is, then pick from that list. The best coffee gift for dad matches how he already makes coffee, not how you think he should.

The dad who brews at home every morning
  • A bag of freshly roasted beans (ground, unless he owns a grinder)
  • A burr grinder, if he doesn't have one. It's one of the most common upgrades for home coffee
  • A pour-over cone and filters for slow weekend cups
The commuter dad
  • An insulated travel mug that fits his car's cup holder (check the base size)
  • Single-serve pour-over packets for the office
  • A refillable cup for the coffee shop he already goes to
The dad who has everything
  • A coffee subscription gift: new coffees by mail, and he picks his taste when he redeems it. Compare the main coffee subscription gifts
  • A coffee from a roaster in a place that matters to him (where he grew up, or a trip you took together)
The gadget dad
  • A coffee scale with a timer
  • A gooseneck kettle for pour-over
  • A handheld milk frother, if he drinks lattes
The "just a good cup" dad
  • A big, heavy mug and a bag of a medium roast from a local roaster
  • A small basket: coffee, mug, a treat, a card. What to put in a coffee gift basket

Three quick checks before you buy

  • Grinder? No grinder means buy ground coffee, never whole bean.
  • Machine? Pods only fit his exact machine. When in doubt, skip pods.
  • Milk or black? Milk drinkers get more out of a frother or a darker roast. Black-coffee drinkers get more out of a lighter, single-origin roast.
