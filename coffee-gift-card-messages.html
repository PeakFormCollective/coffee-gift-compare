Coffee Gift Card Messages: 40 Things to Write

Short answer: keep it to one line, add a coffee pun only if they'd laugh at it, and sign your name. Pick one below by who the gift is for.

For a teacher

  • Thank you for a year of patience. This one's for the mornings that needed extra.
  • You fuel our kids' minds. Let us fuel yours.
  • For the teacher who makes every day worth showing up for. Enjoy a quiet cup.
  • Thanks a latte for everything you do.
  • Grades come and go. Your kindness stays. Enjoy the coffee.
  • To a teacher who's brewing great things. Thank you!

For a coworker or boss

  • Thanks for always having my back, and now your coffee has yours.
  • For all the meetings that should've been emails. Cheers.
  • Working with you is the best part of the day. The coffee is second.
  • A small thank-you for a big year of teamwork.
  • Here's to fewer Mondays and better coffee.
  • You make this place better. Enjoy a well-earned cup.

For dad

  • For the man who taught me that coffee is a food group. Love you, Dad.
  • Dad: first cup's on me this year.
  • To the best dad, with fresh coffee and the same old jokes.
  • You were up early for us for years. Now enjoy it.
  • Happy birthday, Dad. Brew something good.
  • Thanks for every early morning. Here's to slower ones.

For mom

  • Mom, you deserve a cup you get to finish while it's hot.
  • For the woman who ran on coffee and love. Thank you.
  • A little something for your morning ritual. Love you.
  • You've earned a quiet coffee and a long sit-down.
  • To my favorite coffee date.

For a partner

  • You're my favorite part of every morning.
  • Like coffee, you make every day better.
  • I love you a latte. (Sorry. Not sorry.)
  • For our slow Sunday mornings. More of those, please.
  • Here's to a year of coffee in bed.

For a friend

  • Coffee and friends: the perfect blend.
  • For all the conversations we've had over coffee, and all the ones to come.
  • You're brew-tiful, inside and out.
  • Thanks for always being there. Let's catch up over a cup soon.
  • Happy birthday! May your coffee be strong and your Monday short.

Short ones that fit any card

  • Thanks a latte.
  • Brewed with love.
  • Sip happens. Enjoy!
  • Espresso yourself.
  • Have a brew-tiful day.
  • You're the cream in my coffee.
  • Rise and grind.
